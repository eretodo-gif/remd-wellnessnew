Work-related injury care

Support after a workplace injury

Work-related injuries can affect comfort, mobility and the ability to perform daily tasks. ReMD offers a clinical starting point for understanding your symptoms and possible care options.

What to expect

Workplace symptoms may include back and neck discomfort, repetitive-use complaints or other musculoskeletal concerns. A medical evaluation considers the injury history, symptoms and impact on function.

Preparing for your visit

  • Bring a current medication list and relevant prior medical records, if available.
  • Be ready to describe symptoms, activity limitations and when the injury occurred.
  • Ask our scheduling team what records or authorizations may be required.

Questions before scheduling?

If your visit relates to workers’ compensation, ask the office which referral, claim and authorization information is needed. Coverage and reporting rules vary; do not assume a claim is accepted or a specific service is covered.
