Sports injury care

Recovery for active lives

Whether your activity is golf, running, recreational sports or training, an injury can disrupt how you move. ReMD evaluates musculoskeletal complaints and discusses potential next steps.

What to expect

Common reasons to seek an assessment include shoulder, knee, back or neck pain, strains and persistent limitations following activity. Early evaluation can help clarify which symptoms need further medical attention.

Preparing for your visit

  • Bring a current medication list and relevant prior medical records, if available.
  • Be ready to describe symptoms, activity limitations and when the injury occurred.
  • Ask our scheduling team what records or authorizations may be required.

Questions before scheduling?

Return-to-activity decisions are individualized. The website does not replace a diagnosis or clearance to participate in sports.
