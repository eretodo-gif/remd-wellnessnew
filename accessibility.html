Accessibility

Website accessibility and assistance

ReMD Wellness and Injury Center, Inc. is working to make our website easier to use for everyone, including people with disabilities.

Need assistance?

If you have difficulty viewing information, navigating the website, or accessing a page or resource, please call 510-781-0211. Tell us which page or feature was difficult to use and what support you need. We will work with you to provide the information or an alternative means of communication.

Our approach

Our site is being improved using the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AA as a technical target. Accessibility is an ongoing process, and we welcome feedback. This statement is not a certification of complete conformance.

Medical information and emergencies

Do not send sensitive medical details through public website messages. In an emergency, call 911.
