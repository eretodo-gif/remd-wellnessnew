Frequently asked questions

Clear answers to common questions about injury evaluations, office locations and scheduling.

What types of injuries does ReMD focus on?

The practice focuses on patients reporting workplace injuries, sports-related complaints and symptoms after motor-vehicle collisions, including related pain and function concerns.

Where is the main ReMD office?

The main practice address provided is 200 Porter Drive, Suite 210, San Ramon, CA 94583. Additional offices are listed on the Locations page.

How do I request an appointment?

Call 510-781-0211 to ask about appointments, office-specific availability, referrals and what records to bring.

Do I need a referral or workers’ compensation authorization?

Requirements vary by visit type, payer and claim circumstances. Call the office for guidance before your visit.

Does ReMD provide Qualified Medical Evaluator services?

Albert V. Retodo, MD is identified by the practice as a Qualified Medical Evaluator. Please call to confirm current QME status, eligibility, process and scheduling.

What should I do after a serious accident?

For severe or emergency symptoms after an accident, seek immediate emergency medical attention or call 911. Website information is not a substitute for urgent assessment.
